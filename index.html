# Analyze This Info ### Business Analytics & Consulting **Your data has a story. Let's find it.** Analyze This Info is a business analytics consulting service focused on turning the data businesses already have into clear, meaningful insights. I analyze business data to uncover trends, identify opportunities, and help organizations better understand what is happening inside their business. ## What I Analyze ### Employee Turnover Identify patterns in employee retention and turnover, including trends by department, position, tenure, and other available business factors. ### Marketing Performance Evaluate marketing activity to understand which campaigns, channels, and efforts are producing meaningful results. ### Customer Retention Analyze customer behavior to identify retention patterns, repeat business, and areas where customer relationships may be strengthened. ### Revenue & Profitability Examine revenue, expenses, margins, and other financial performance indicators to identify trends and potential opportunities. ### Operations Analyze operational data to identify inefficiencies, bottlenecks, performance trends, and opportunities to improve processes. ## How It Works ### 1. Discover We begin with a consultation to understand the business question you are trying to answer. ### 2. Define We identify the relevant data, metrics, KPIs, and scope of the analysis. ### 3. Analyze You provide the appropriate business data. The data is reviewed, cleaned, validated, and analyzed. ### 4. Visualize Key findings are transformed into clear charts, dashboards, and visualizations. ### 5. Report A professional report summarizes the analysis, notable findings, trends, and areas that may warrant further investigation. ### 6. Present The results are presented directly to the client so the findings can be understood in context and discussed together. ## Services - Business Data Analysis - Employee Turnover Analysis - Marketing Performance Analysis - Customer Retention Analysis - Revenue & Profitability Analysis - Operational Analytics - KPI Analysis - Data Visualization - Custom Business Reports - Dashboard Development - Analytics Presentations ## Tools & Technologies This project and its analytics work may utilize: - SQL - Microsoft Excel - Google Sheets - Tableau - R - BigQuery - Data Visualization - Data Cleaning & Validation - Business Analysis - Process Analysis ## About the Project This repository contains the website and supporting materials for Analyze This Info, a business analytics and consulting service. The website is designed to provide prospective clients with information about available services, the analytics process, example analyses, and consultation options. ## Website [Visit Analyze This Info](https://jessicatolton925-jpg.github.io/analyze-this-info/) ## Project Goals The goal of Analyze This Info is to make business data easier to understand and more useful for decision-making. Businesses often have valuable information sitting inside spreadsheets, databases, reports, and existing business systems. The purpose of this service is to help transform that information into understandable analysis and visual evidence. ## Privacy & Data Handling Client information should be handled responsibly and only the data necessary for the agreed-upon analysis should be requested. Whenever possible, personally identifiable information should be removed or excluded from analytical datasets. Client data should not be included in this public repository. ## Disclaimer Analytics findings describe patterns and relationships observed in the data provided. They should be considered business insights rather than guarantees of future results.
