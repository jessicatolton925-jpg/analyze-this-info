# Analyze This Info

## Business Analytics & Consulting

**Your data has a story. Let's find it.**

Analyze This Info is a business analytics consulting service focused on turning the data businesses already have into clear, meaningful insights.

The goal is simple: take existing business information, identify useful patterns and trends, and present the findings in a way that helps organizations better understand their performance and make more informed decisions.

## What We Analyze

### Employee Turnover & Retention

Explore turnover patterns, retention trends, tenure, departments, positions, separation reasons, and other available workforce metrics.

### Marketing Performance

Examine campaigns, spending, leads, conversions, customer acquisition, and other available marketing performance metrics.

### Customer Retention

Identify patterns in customer activity, repeat business, retention, churn, and customer behavior.

### Revenue & Profitability

Analyze revenue, expenses, margins, products, services, customers, and other financial performance indicators.

### Operations & Efficiency

Examine workflows, productivity, operational performance, bottlenecks, and process trends to identify areas worth investigating.

### Custom Business Analysis

Have a business question that does not fit neatly into one category? Analyze This Info can help determine what your existing data can reveal.

## How It Works

### 1. Consult

We discuss your business, the question you are trying to answer, the information you already have, and what you hope to learn.

### 2. Analyze

Relevant information is reviewed, organized, validated, and analyzed.

### 3. Visualize

Important findings are transformed into clear charts, graphs, tables, dashboards, and other visual summaries where appropriate.

### 4. Report

Findings are organized into a professional report highlighting meaningful trends, patterns, comparisons, and areas worth further investigation.

### 5. Present

The results are presented to the client so the findings can be understood in context and discussed together.

## Privacy & Data Handling

Client information should be handled responsibly and only the information necessary for the agreed-upon analysis should be requested.

Whenever possible, personally identifiable information should be removed or excluded from analytical datasets.

Client data should never be committed to or stored in this public repository.

## About

Analyze This Info was created to help organizations make better use of the information they already collect without necessarily needing to hire a full-time analyst.

The approach combines business analysis, data analysis, visualization, and process improvement to turn complicated information into understandable insights.

## Disclaimer

Analytics findings describe patterns and relationships observed in the data provided. They should not be interpreted as guarantees of future business results.

Recommendations and areas of opportunity are intended to support business decision-making and should be evaluated within the organization's broader context.
